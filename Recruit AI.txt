# Build an Enterprise AI Recruitment CRM for Staffing Agencies

Build a modern, production-ready SaaS web application called **RecruitFlow AI**.

## Vision

RecruitFlow AI is **not a job portal**.

Candidates continue applying through LinkedIn, Indeed, Dice, Glassdoor, Monster, ZipRecruiter, referrals, and email.

RecruitFlow AI becomes the company's **single source of truth**, replacing Excel sheets, emails, and manual candidate tracking.

---

# Core Problem

Recruitment agencies currently:

* Download resumes from different job portals.
* Store candidate details in Excel.
* Send resumes through email.
* Lose track of interview feedback.
* Create duplicate candidate records.
* Have poor communication between recruiters, HR, SMEs, and managers.

The software should eliminate these problems.
